# Storyboard: Clawd and the butterfly (15 s)

Logline: Clawd wants to catch a butterfly, but it dodges every pounce, so Clawd gives up, and that's when it lands on Clawd's head.

World: one sunny meadow. Sky blue, soft greens, cream daisies, a violet-and-rose butterfly. The light warms across the
video, from a cool late morning (A) to a golden afternoon (C), as Clawd's mood goes from bored, to frustrated, to loved.

Motif: the butterfly landing. It lands on a daisy in the first shot and on Clawd's head in the last.

Clawd's arc: bored → surprised → mischief → dizzy → determined → angry → determined → dizzy → sad → love.
